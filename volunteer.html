Our Expectations

Volunteer Principles

We value respect, reliability, inclusion and responsible conduct.

  • Volunteer roles will be based on the needs of the programme
  • Roles may require an application, orientation and identity verification
  • Reference checks or additional safeguards may apply depending on the activity
  • Volunteers working with children or vulnerable individuals must follow applicable safeguarding policies
