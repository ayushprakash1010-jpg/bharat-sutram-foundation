Trust & Responsibility

Our Commitment to Responsible Giving

We recognise that every donation represents trust. We aim to maintain appropriate financial records, follow applicable legal requirements and communicate programme progress honestly.

Where project-specific donations are accepted, the intended purpose and relevant terms should be explained clearly. Any material changes in use will be handled in accordance with the applicable terms and law.

Before You Donate

  • Please review the Foundation's verified registration details
  • Check applicable donation terms and payment information
  • Review relevant tax documentation
  • Tax deductions are available only where the necessary eligibility and approvals apply

Ready to Support?

To make a donation, please contact the Foundation directly to receive verified registration details, payment information and applicable terms.

We do not currently accept online payments through this website. Payment details and receipt processes will be shared upon enquiry to ensure security and transparency.
