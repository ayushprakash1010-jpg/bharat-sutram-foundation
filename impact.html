Our Impact

Purpose Matters. Progress Must Be Demonstrated.

We track not only how many people participate in our programmes, but also what they learn, what barriers they face and whether the support provided contributes to useful outcomes.

Measuring What Matters

How We Define Progress

🚪

Access

Are intended participants able to access and participate in the programme?

📚

Learning

Do participants demonstrate improvements in relevant knowledge or skills?

🔄

Continuity

Where applicable, are participants able to continue education, pursue further training or access appropriate services?

🚀

Opportunity

Do participants progress towards employment, self-employment or other stated objectives?

💬

Community Feedback

How do participants and relevant stakeholders assess the usefulness, accessibility and quality of our activities?

Honest Reporting

Transparency in Reporting

We communicate our progress through programme updates, annual reports and impact reports as our work develops.

Published information should distinguish between activities planned, activities delivered, outputs achieved and longer-term outcomes.

We will use verified data, explain relevant limitations and avoid presenting estimates or targets as completed results.

Stories of Change

Individual experiences can help illustrate how programmes are experienced by participants. Any stories shared will be based on genuine participation and informed consent.

This section will feature verified stories as programmes are implemented.

Our Commitment

Meaningful social outcomes require time, collaboration and continued learning. We approach our work with purpose, measure progress responsibly and share what we learn so that programmes improve over time.
