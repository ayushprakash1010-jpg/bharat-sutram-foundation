Financial Accountability

Responsible Financial Management

We aim to maintain appropriate financial records and follow applicable accounting, reporting and audit requirements.

Where available and appropriate, we intend to publish annual financial statements, audit reports, programme budgets and other relevant documents.

Project-specific funding will be managed in accordance with applicable terms, donor restrictions and legal requirements.

Programme Accountability

Measuring What We Deliver

We aim to define objectives, document programme activities and track suitable outputs and outcomes.

Programme reports will distinguish planned activities from completed work and intended outcomes from verified results.

Ethics & Safety

Safeguarding & Ethical Practice

We aim to protect the dignity, safety and privacy of people participating in our programmes. Relevant policies will address appropriate conduct, child safeguarding, privacy, consent, complaints and the handling of concerns.

Our programme design and delivery will be guided by applicable legal requirements and appropriate professional standards.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Bharat Sutram Foundation is a social development initiative focused on expanding access to education, skill development, girls' and women's empowerment, community health awareness and senior citizen welfare. Please refer to our About Us page for verified organisational and registration information.
Our proposed programme areas include digital and vocational skills, educational support for children, girls' empowerment, community health awareness and digital inclusion for senior citizens. Programme availability will depend on implementation plans, resources and suitable partnerships.
Eligibility will vary by initiative. Each programme will specify its intended beneficiaries, age criteria, location and selection process before enrolment.
You may explore donation opportunities, volunteering, professional expertise, training partnerships or institutional collaboration. Contributions and participation are subject to applicable requirements and programme needs.
Companies can contact us to explore suitable CSR or other institutional partnerships. Any CSR project will be subject to the Foundation's eligibility, applicable regulations, project readiness and the requirements of the prospective partner.
International supporters may contact us to explore lawful partnership options. Any foreign contribution must comply with applicable Indian laws and required registrations or permissions. Please contact the Foundation before initiating an international transfer.
Tax benefits depend on the Foundation's applicable registrations, approvals, donor eligibility and prevailing law. Please rely only on verified information and valid documentation provided by the Foundation.
We aim to track relevant programme activities, participation, learning outcomes and other suitable indicators. Verified results and available organisational reports will be published as the work progresses. Visit our Impact page to learn more.
Please visit our Volunteer page and submit the interest form with information about your skills, availability and preferred area of contribution. Volunteer opportunities depend on programme needs and applicable safeguarding requirements.
Please contact us with your organisation's details, proposed area of collaboration and any relevant project or funding requirements. We can then assess whether there is a suitable basis for further discussion.
