Partner With Us

Collaborating to Expand Opportunity and Social Impact

We develop partnerships with clear objectives, strong governance and shared understanding of outcomes.

Partnership Opportunities

Ways to Collaborate

Corporate CSR Partnerships

Businesses can explore supporting eligible programmes through funding, training resources, employee volunteering, equipment, technical expertise and institutional collaboration.

Potential Areas of Collaboration

  • Digital literacy and skill development
  • Education support for children
  • Girls' education and empowerment
  • Vocational learning and livelihood initiatives
  • Community health awareness
  • Digital inclusion for senior citizens

Projects will be developed according to community needs, organisational capacity, applicable CSR requirements and the interests of prospective partners.

Philanthropic & Foundation Partnerships

We welcome discussions with philanthropic organisations, charitable foundations and individual donors interested in supporting carefully designed community development initiatives.

Potential Collaborations

  • Programme funding and pilot projects
  • Capacity building and learning resources
  • Multi-year support where appropriate

We welcome interest from international foundations, diaspora networks and organisations seeking responsible partnerships for community development in India. Any international funding will be subject to applicable Indian laws and necessary approvals.

Educational & Training Partnerships

Schools, colleges, training institutions and qualified professionals can help strengthen programme design and delivery.

Collaboration May Include

  • Learning resources and trainer support
  • Career guidance and mentoring
  • Access to appropriate educational facilities
Project Framework

Implementation & Reporting

For suitable projects, we aim to develop a project proposal outlining:

  • The identified need and target beneficiaries
  • Proposed activities and implementation schedule
  • Expected outputs and outcomes
  • Budget and intended use of funds
  • Monitoring and reporting arrangements
  • Relevant risks, safeguards and responsibilities

Our ability to implement a project will depend on its scope, available capacity, funding and the suitability of proposed partners.
